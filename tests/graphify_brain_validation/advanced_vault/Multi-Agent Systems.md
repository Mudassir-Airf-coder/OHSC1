# Multi-Agent Systems

Multi-Agent Systems coordinate several specialized [[AI Agent|AI Agents]] — such as a [[Planner]], an [[Orchestrator]], and a [[Reviewer]] — to solve problems beyond any single agent. They rely on shared memory, often a [[Knowledge Graph]], and on message-passing protocols.

These systems are an application of [[Software Architecture]] and are frequently exposed to clients through an [[MCP]] interface. Their emergent behavior is studied under [[Evaluation]].
