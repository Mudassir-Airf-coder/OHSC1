# Reinforcement Learning

Reinforcement Learning (RL) is a branch of [[Machine Learning]] where an agent learns a policy by maximizing cumulative reward from interactions with an environment. It underlies robotics, game play, and alignment of [[LLMs]] (RLHF).

RL connects to [[AI Agent|AI Agents]] through shared concepts of policy and reward, and its results are tracked via [[Evaluation]]. It sits alongside [[Deep Learning]] as a core training paradigm.
