# Knowledge Management

Knowledge Management is the discipline of capturing, organizing, and retrieving organizational knowledge. Personal knowledge management (PKM) is practiced in tools like [[Obsidian]], where [[Wikilinks]] and [[Knowledge Graph|knowledge graphs]] help users build a second brain.

Effective knowledge management combines human-authored structure with automated extraction by tools like [[Graphify]] and is increasingly augmented by [[AI Agent|AI Agents]]. It underpins [[Software Architecture]] decisions in engineering organizations.
