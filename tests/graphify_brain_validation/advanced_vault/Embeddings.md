# Embeddings

Embeddings are dense vector representations of tokens, sentences, or entities produced by [[Neural Networks]] and [[Deep Learning]]. They are the foundation of [[Vector Search]] and [[Semantic Retrieval]], and they bridge unstructured text with the structured world of a [[Knowledge Graph]].

Embeddings let [[Graphify]] compare concepts and let [[AI Agent|AI Agents]] reason over similarity. They are a core artifact of [[Machine Learning]].
