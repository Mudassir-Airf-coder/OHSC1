# Retrieval Augmented Generation

Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG) grounds an [[LLMs|LLM]] in external evidence fetched by [[Semantic Retrieval]] and [[Vector Search]]. RAG reduces hallucination by injecting a [[Knowledge Graph]] or corpus excerpt into the prompt.

RAG is the dominant pattern for [[AI Agent|AI Agents]] that answer over private data such as an [[Obsidian]] vault, and it is validated by [[Evaluation]].
