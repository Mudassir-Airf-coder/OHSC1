# Graph Engineering

Graph Engineering is the discipline of designing, building, querying, and maintaining graph-structured data systems. It spans schema design, extraction (e.g. with [[Graphify]]), storage, and analytics such as community detection and shortest-path.

Graph engineering underpins [[Knowledge Graph]] construction, supports [[Artificial Intelligence]] reasoning, and is how practitioners scale an [[Obsidian]] vault into a queryable network. It also informs how [[AI Agent|AI Agents]] share and traverse structured memory, and is foundational to [[Graph Databases]].
