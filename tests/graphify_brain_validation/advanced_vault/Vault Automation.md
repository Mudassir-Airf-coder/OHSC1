# Vault Automation

Vault Automation refers to scripts and agents that manage an [[Obsidian]] vault programmatically — creating notes, maintaining wikilinks, building maps of content, and running [[Graphify]] extraction on a schedule.

Automation is orchestrated by the [[Orchestrator]] and governed by the [[Reviewer]] to keep the vault safe. It is a concrete application of [[Graph Engineering]] ideas to personal knowledge management, closely tied to the [[Knowledge Graph]] of the vault.
