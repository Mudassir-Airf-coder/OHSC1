# Evaluation

Evaluation is the disciplined measurement of how well a system meets its goals. In AI, [[Evaluation]] scores [[LLMs]] and [[AI Agent|AI Agents]] on accuracy, faithfulness, and safety, using benchmarks and human review.

Evaluation informs the [[Reviewer]] quality gate in agent systems and validates [[Machine Learning]] models. It is a cross-cutting concern spanning [[Software Architecture]] and [[Knowledge Management]].
