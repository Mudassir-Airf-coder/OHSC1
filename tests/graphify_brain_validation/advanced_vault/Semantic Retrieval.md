# Semantic Retrieval

Semantic Retrieval is the task of fetching knowledge based on meaning. It builds on [[Vector Search]] and on the structured relationships in a [[Knowledge Graph]], giving [[AI Agent|AI Agents]] the ability to answer questions over a private corpus such as an [[Obsidian]] vault.

Graphify-derived graphs and [[Wikilinks]] both contribute signals to semantic retrieval, which the [[Orchestrator]] uses when servicing user queries. It is a practical intersection of [[Machine Learning]] and [[Graph Engineering]].
