# Neural Networks

Neural Networks are parameterized function approximators inspired by biological neurons. They are the architectural backbone of deep learning, a branch of [[Machine Learning]]. Stacking many layers yields deep networks capable of representation learning from raw signals.

Neural networks power the reasoning and language capabilities of modern [[AI Agent|AI Agents]] and are frequently trained on corpora whose structure is described by a [[Knowledge Graph]]. Their learned representations can be visualized and analyzed using [[Graph Engineering]] techniques.
