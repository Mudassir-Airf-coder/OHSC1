# Knowledge Graph

A Knowledge Graph is a graph-structured knowledge base that stores entities (nodes) and the relationships (edges) between them. Knowledge graphs power semantic search, recommendation, and the reasoning used by [[AI Agent|AI Agents]].

In the context of [[Artificial Intelligence]], a knowledge graph complements [[Machine Learning]] by providing explicit, queryable structure. Tools like [[Graphify]] automatically extract knowledge graphs from documents, while [[Obsidian]] lets humans author them through [[Wikilinks]]. Designing and maintaining such graphs is the concern of [[Graph Engineering]].
