# Wikilinks

Wikilinks are Obsidian's `[[note name]]` syntax for linking notes. They form the explicit, human-authored backbone of a personal [[Knowledge Graph]] inside [[Obsidian]].

Unlike the inferred edges that [[Graphify]] extracts from text, wikilinks are intentional assertions of relatedness. Both together — explicit wikilinks and implicit semantic links — feed [[Graph Engineering]] and give [[AI Agent|AI Agents]] a richer picture of a knowledge base.
