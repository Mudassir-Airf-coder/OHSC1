# Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the field of computer science concerned with building systems that can perform tasks requiring human intelligence, such as reasoning, learning, and perception. Modern AI is driven by [[Machine Learning]], which trains statistical models from data, and by [[Neural Networks]], which model computation after the brain.

AI systems increasingly act as [[AI Agent|AI Agents]] that perceive an environment and take goal-directed actions. To reason effectively, agents organize knowledge in a [[Knowledge Graph]], which represents entities and their relationships. The practical tooling for managing such graphs in personal knowledge bases includes [[Obsidian]] and the [[Graphify]] extraction engine.

AI is also closely related to [[Graph Engineering]], the discipline of designing and querying graph-structured data.
